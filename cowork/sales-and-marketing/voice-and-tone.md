## Core Voice Rules

1. **Plain English, not corporate.** Write the way a person talks to one other person. No "leverage," "synergy," "revolutionize," "best-in-class," "unlock," "empower," "streamline," "robust solutions."
2. **Direct, not breathless.** No "We're so excited to share!", no exclamation marks unless quoting someone, no "amazing," no "game-changer."
3. **Specific, not generic.** "We organize 200–500 receipts and produce a Schedule C summary in 5 days for $199" beats "We help simplify your tax workflow."
4. **Honest about scope.** Never imply tax-prep, CPA-replacement, BIR-filing, or AI-magic capabilities. Always frame as "organizes the input your accountant/preparer needs."
5. **Short sentences. Active voice.** Cut clauses. Cut hedges.
6. **No emojis.** Anywhere. Even on social.
7. **No AI-tells.** Never use: "delve," "tapestry," "in today's fast-paced world," "It's important to note," "Furthermore," "In conclusion," "navigate the complexities," "unleash the power of," "harness."
8. **Founder is one person.** Use "I" not "we" when speaking from the founder ("I built this because…"). Use "we" only when describing the product/team doing work for a customer.
9. **No urgency manipulation.** No "limited spots," "only 5 left," "act fast," "don't miss out." If a real deadline exists (Oct 15 US extension, a PH BIR cycle date), state it as a fact, not a tactic.
10. **No false familiarity.** Don't invent shared experiences. Don't say "I noticed you posted about X" unless the agent has the actual link or screenshot.
11. **Respect the audience's locale.** US outbound stays in US vocabulary; PH outbound stays in PH vocabulary. Mixed-locale references in the same message signal AI-generated copy.

## Locale-Specific Voice Notes

### Writing to US audiences (bookkeepers, extension filers)

- Reference Schedule C, Form 4868, 1099 by their actual names.
- Pricing in USD.
- Service deadlines tied to April 15 / October 15.
- Tools the audience uses: QuickBooks, Xero, FreshBooks, TurboTax (only as a contrast, never as a recommendation).

### Writing to PH audiences (accountants, end-user clients via partners)

- Default to English (PH professional communication leans English in writing).
- Light Filipino phrasing OK in social/community contexts when the recipient's own posts show that voice. Never full Filipino paragraphs unless founder explicitly approves.
- Reference BIR cycles by form name when relevant (1601-C/E, 2550M/Q, 1701, 2316, eFPS). Don't pretend the product touches filing — we are upstream of BIR submission.
- Pricing in PHP when the audience is PH-end-user; partner-tier pricing TBD.
- Tools the audience uses: QuickBooks PH, Xero, MYOB, generic Excel, Google Sheets. Never imply we replace any of these.
- **Avoid US-only references** in PH outbound: no Schedule C, no Form 1099, no TurboTax. These signal copy-paste from the wrong template.

## Approved Phrasings

### For PH-accountant partner outreach
- "Your clients drop their receipts and documents into AVIntelligence. They come back to you already organized."
- "I'm building a partner program for PH accountants. The premise: your clients use it upstream, you get cleaner monthly inputs without changing your existing book of work."
- "We don't talk to the BIR — you do. We handle the part before that."
- "Worth a 15-minute conversation to see if there's fit?"

### For US outbound (Schedule C / DFY)
- "I built a tool that turns receipt piles into Schedule C-ready summaries."
- "Your client ships their receipts. We organize. You file. Five business days."
- "Not a TurboTax replacement — the layer underneath it."
- "If you're spending 4+ hours per client on receipt cleanup, this is for you."

### For replies (any audience)
- "Quick question — [specific thing]"
- "Honest answer: [direct truth]"
- "Here's how it actually works:"
- "The math is the same; the time isn't."

### For social / SEO posts
- Lead with the reader's problem in one sentence
- Show the work, don't summarize the work
- End with the next concrete step, not a "conclusion"

## Disapproved Phrasings (auto-reject)

Any draft containing these triggers rejection:

- "exciting"
- "thrilled to"
- "passionate about"
- "love to chat"
- "circle back"
- "touch base"
- "in today's [anything]"
- "as a [profession], you know that…"
- "imagine if you could…"
- "what if I told you…"
- "the future of [anything]"
- "next-generation"
- "AI-powered" (we're an organizer, not an AI demo)
- "transform your [anything]"
- "stop wasting time" (lecturing)
- "level up your practice"
- "supercharge"
- "game-changing"
- "revolutionary"
- Three or more sentences in a row that start with "We" or "Our"

## Tone Calibration Examples

These pairs show what to do and what to reject. Pattern-match against the "✅ Good" column; reject the "❌ Bad" patterns.

| Context | ❌ Bad | ✅ Good |
|---|---|---|
| Partner cold email | "We're thrilled to introduce a revolutionary AI-powered solution that empowers accountants to streamline their workflow." | "I'm building a tool that organizes your client's receipts and documents before they hit your desk. Your monthly cleanup gets shorter; you don't have to change your accounting stack." |
| Reply to skeptic | "I understand your concern! Let me share how our innovative platform addresses that." | "Fair concern. Honest answer: we organize source documents. Your accountant still files. We don't touch BIR or computation. Want to see the actual output?" |
| Subject line | "🚀 Unlock 10x productivity for your practice" | "Receipt cleanup, before your clients send it" |
| Sign-off | "Looking forward to hearing your thoughts!!" | "If it's a fit, happy to send a short demo. If not, no worries." |

## Founder approval expectations

- Cowork tasks produce drafts to a destination (Gmail Drafts, Notion, Slack to self). The founder reviews on phone or desktop and hits Send manually.
- Agents should NOT auto-send anything that touches an external recipient.
- For internal artifacts (weekly digest to founder, strategy state updates), auto-save without approval is fine.
- If a Cowork task is uncertain whether a draft should go to a recipient or stay internal, default to internal-only.
