# AVIntelligence — Growth Playbook + Research Prompt Pack

*Goal: 200 paying subs (get-by MRR), then 1,000+. Constraint: founder is strong at build/design, weak at cold outreach. So we select for channels that are a BUILD problem, not a SALES problem. Ads, Product Hunt, Show HN, Reddit, and cold accountant email have already failed — do not repeat them as primary channels.*

---

## The bet (why these channels)

People don't buy avintph because they don't *find* it at the moment they have the problem. Fix = be where they already search with intent, using assets you can build once and that compound. No charisma required.

## Prioritized plan (highest leverage first)

1. **SEO content engine (evergreen + seasonal).** Publish authoritative guides for high-intent queries buyers actually type: "how to organize receipts for taxes," "1099 expense tracker," "Schedule C categories explained," "how to prep documents for my accountant." Compounds monthly; peaks at tax season.
2. **Comparison / "alternative" pages.** Bottom-funnel intent: "Shoeboxed alternative," "Dext for freelancers," "Keeper vs [you]," "best receipt organizer for landlords." People searching these are ready to switch/buy.
3. **Get into the roundups that already rank.** The "best receipt scanner / expense tracker / tax-prep app 2026" listicles send real buyers. Email those authors, offer free Pro + a 10-min demo, ask to be included/reviewed. ~30 targeted, warm emails — leverage, not cold sales.
4. **Free-tool funnel (product-led SEO).** Ship a genuinely useful *free* micro-tool that ranks and funnels to Pro — e.g., free "receipt → clean CSV," "Schedule C category finder," or "1099 income organizer." The tool is the ad; upgrade for the full engine. This is a build task — your strength.
5. **Programmatic SEO.** Auto-generate many long-tail pages (per profession, per expense type, per state) from your data/engine. Scales content without hand-writing each page — again, a build task.
6. **Seasonal tax push (Jan–Apr).** Position the $6 Day Pass as the no-commitment tax-time entry ("get your accountant-ready bundle for $6"), converting to Pro. Seed 1–5 NOW so they rank by January.
7. **Light affiliate / referral.** Give finance bloggers, YouTubers, and happy users a cut. Turns other people into your distribution without you doing outreach at scale.

## Sequencing to the numbers

- **First ~200:** roundup inclusions (#3) + your best 8–12 SEO/comparison pages (#1, #2) + free tool (#4), timed to tax season (#6).
- **200 → 1,000:** compounding SEO + programmatic pages (#5) + affiliate flywheel (#7). Double down on whichever page/tool actually converts.
- **Watch:** which channel produced each paying user (add a "how did you hear" field). Pour fuel on the winner only.

## Perplexity / research prompt pack (paste these; run them yourself)

**A. Keyword map (intent + difficulty)**
> "Act as an SEO strategist. I run a US web app that uses AI to organize financial/tax documents (receipts, invoices, payslips, 1099s) into structured, accountant-ready reports and dashboards, priced $12/mo. List 40 high-intent, low-to-medium-competition search queries US freelancers, sole proprietors, gig workers, and small landlords type when they need this. For each: estimated monthly US search volume, competition level, search intent (informational/commercial/transactional), and the page type I should build (guide, tool, or comparison). Group by funnel stage."

**B. Roundup / listicle targets to get listed in**
> "Find the top 20 currently-ranking articles (US, 2026) for 'best receipt scanner app,' 'best expense tracker for freelancers,' 'best tax prep tools for self-employed,' and 'receipt organizer for small business.' For each: the URL, the publication/author, whether they appear to accept new tools or affiliates, and the best contact method. Prioritize ones a small tool could realistically get added to."

**C. Competitor gaps to exploit in comparison pages**
> "Summarize the top complaints and missing features of Shoeboxed, Dext, Keeper Tax, and QuickBooks Self-Employed, based on 2025–2026 user reviews (G2, Capterra, Reddit, Trustpilot). For each competitor, give me the 3 sharpest weaknesses I could position against for a $12/mo AI document-organization + insights tool that produces an accountant-ready bundle but does NOT file taxes."

**D. Communities with intent (that allow participation)**
> "List US online communities (forums, Facebook groups, Discords, Slacks, niche newsletters — NOT Reddit) where freelancers, gig workers, sole proprietors, and small landlords ask about organizing receipts, tracking expenses, and prepping for taxes. For each: size, how active, and whether helpful vendor participation is tolerated or against the rules."

**E. Free-tool funnel ideas (product-led SEO)**
> "Identify 10 free single-purpose web-tool ideas in the US tax/receipt/expense/document space that (a) have real monthly search demand, (b) are simple to build, and (c) naturally upsell to a paid AI document-organization + reporting product. For each: the target search query, rough volume, and how it funnels to the paid product."

**F. Programmatic SEO patterns**
> "Give me 8 scalable long-tail SEO page templates for a US AI tax/document tool where I can auto-generate hundreds of pages from one template (e.g., by profession, expense category, US state, or document type). For each pattern: the URL/title formula, the search intent it captures, and an example query it would rank for."

**G. Affiliate / creator partners**
> "Find US finance/freelancer/small-business YouTubers, bloggers, and newsletter writers (2026) who review tax, bookkeeping, or receipt tools and run affiliate programs or accept sponsored reviews. For each: audience size, platform, typical content, and how to pitch a small SaaS."

---

## What to do with the outputs

Feed A/E/F back to *me* (or Codex) as a build brief — the SEO pages, free tool, and programmatic templates are code/design work you're good at. Use B/G to send ~30 warm inclusion/affiliate emails. Use C to write the comparison pages. Keep it to one or two channels until one clearly converts, then scale that.
