## Slide 6 — Spotlight: AI workflow automation ⭐
- The pattern: **review/validate → decide → record → notify** — put an AI where a human
  bottleneck sits today.
- **Case — PicklePal partner-onboarding review:**
  - Reads venue-partner applications from a Google Sheet.
  - AI (Claude) validates ID, authority, entity, and role documents — with image-quality checks
    so it never guesses from an unreadable file — and scores each 0–100 with a decision
    (approve / review / reject) and reviewer notes.
  - Decisions are written back to the sheet and applicants are emailed automatically.
- Simple flow diagram: **Sheet → AI validation → parse → record + build email → send.**
- Takeaway: what took manual reviewing per applicant now runs itself, consistently and on the
  record.
