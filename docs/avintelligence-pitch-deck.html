01
Problem

Financial records are everywhere, but understanding them is still manual.

  • Receipts, invoices, payslips, contracts, and screenshots live across folders, drives, email, and chat.
  • Most people do not have structured records when they need reporting, review, or tax preparation.
  • Storage tools keep files, but do not convert them into usable financial context.

Core problem

People store financial documents, but they still cannot work with them as living financial data.

1

Scattered files

Records live across multiple apps and storage habits, with no single system of record.

2

Manual cleanup

Users still rename, sort, inspect, and reconcile documents by hand before any reporting starts.

3

Delayed insight

By the time documents become understandable, the moment for fast decision-making is already gone.

Simple truth: file retention is not the same as financial understanding.
02
Solution

AVIntelligence converts financial documents into a usable workspace.

Not just OCR. Not just storage. Not just Dashboards. One workflow from document intake to financial understanding.

Smart Storage

Organizes uploads into one account-isolated document workspace.

Structured Extraction

Turns files into fields, normalized records, and usable financial data.

Reports + Dashboards

Creates summaries, analytics visuals, contextual insight, and drill-down views.

Workflow

Upload -> Extract -> Report -> Understand

Account-isolated, server-side workflow
