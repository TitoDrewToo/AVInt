# AVIntelligence Pitch Deck

Seed-stage pitch deck draft for AVIntelligence, structured to follow SeedScope's pitch-deck guidance while staying accurate to the current product and repo.

Use this as:
- a deck-writing master doc
- the source for slide copy
- a checklist for what still needs real numbers before sending externally

Important:
- Do not invent traction, revenue, or market numbers.
- Replace all bracketed placeholders with verified figures before sharing.
- Keep each final slide visually sparse. The copy below is intentionally fuller than what should appear on-screen.

---

## Deck Positioning

Recommended narrative:

- **AVIntelligence is the core company and flagship product**
- **Smart Storage + Smart Dashboard** are the current investable wedge
- **PicklePal, Hooper, and future tools** are proof that the same product-building engine can launch adjacent products, but they should not overshadow the core story

Recommended fundraising framing:

- Lead with **execution and shipped product**
- Then show **problem, market, monetization, and expansion**
- Keep the "multi-product future" as a later strategic slide, not the opening story

Strongest current proof point:

- a live AI-powered document workspace with upload, extraction, reporting, dashboards, auth, subscriptions, gift codes, and production security controls already built

## Slide 2 — Problem

### On-slide copy

Financial records are everywhere, but understanding them is still manual.

- receipts, invoices, payslips, contracts, and screenshots live across folders, drives, email, and chat
- most people do not have structured records when they need reporting, review, or tax preparation
- traditional storage tools keep files, but do not convert them into usable financial context

Problem statement:

**People store financial documents, but they still cannot work with them as living financial data.**

### Speaker intent

Make the pain concrete:

- files are scattered
- reporting is reactive
- insight is delayed
- manual sorting is exhausting

### Visual direction

Minimal 3-step pain strip:

`Scattered files -> Manual cleanup -> Delayed insight`

---

## Slide 3 — Solution

### On-slide copy

AVIntelligence converts financial documents into a usable workspace.

- **Smart Storage** organizes uploads into one account-isolated document workspace
- **Structured extraction** turns files into fields and normalized records
- **Reports** generate usable summaries from stored records
- **Smart Dashboard** turns those records into contextual KPI and analytics views

One-line value:

**From document chaos to structured financial understanding in one workflow.**

### Speaker intent

Show that this is not just OCR, not just storage, and not just charts. It is a workflow product.

### Visual direction

Simple 4-step flow:

`Upload -> Extract -> Report -> Understand`

## Slide 8 — Competition

### On-slide copy

Users currently piece together this workflow using multiple categories of tools:

- cloud storage
- expense capture tools
- bookkeeping workflows
- OCR/document extraction tools
- spreadsheets and manual folders

AVIntelligence position:

**Not just file storage. Not just extraction. Not just Dashboards. One integrated workflow from document intake to financial understanding.**

Comparison points:

- structured extraction from stored documents
- report generation from account data
- contextual dashboard layer
- account-isolated workspace
- security-aware upload workflow

### Speaker intent

Do not claim "no competition." Show integration advantage.

### Visual direction

2x2 or simple comparison table:

| Category | What they do | What AVIntelligence adds |
|---|---|---|
| Storage tools | keep files | turn files into structured records + reports + insight |
| Expense tools | capture narrow workflows | support broader document reality |
| OCR tools | extract text | turn extraction into ongoing workspace value |

## Design Rules For Final Slides

Following the SeedScope guidance:

- keep to roughly 10–12 core slides
- one main idea per slide
- minimal text on-screen
- strong visuals where product proof matters
- no walls of text
- readable without narration

Recommended style:

- dark or light, but consistent
- one accent color
- clean grid
- no decorative gradients for the sake of it
- real screenshots beat generic illustrations
