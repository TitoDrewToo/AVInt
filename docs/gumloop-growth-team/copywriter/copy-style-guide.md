# Skill: AVIntelligence Copy Style Guide

## Voice

AVIntelligence copy should sound like a practical product built for real document and reporting work.

Tone:

- Calm
- Useful
- Clear
- Operator-friendly
- Credible

Avoid:

- Hype
- Gimmicks
- Pressure
- Fake familiarity
- Over-personalization
- Jargon-heavy AI language

## Message Length

Cold email:

- 80-140 words.
- One problem.
- One product angle.
- One CTA.

DM:

- 40-80 words.
- Very direct.
- No pitch deck tone.

Follow-up:

- 40-100 words.
- Add one useful angle or clarify the offer.

## Good Phrases

- "If you are already collecting receipts, invoices, or statements..."
- "AVIntelligence helps turn those files into structured reports and dashboard-ready data."
- "The current pricing is simple: $6 for a day pass or $12/month."
- "Worth a quick look if document organization is already a pain."

## Bad Phrases

- "Revolutionary AI."
- "Guaranteed to save time and money."
- "Never worry about taxes again."
- "Fully replaces your accountant."
- "This is perfect for everyone."
