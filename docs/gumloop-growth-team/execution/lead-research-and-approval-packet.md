# Skill: Lead Research And Approval Packet

## Research Rules

Use only available and reasonable business context.

Look for:

- Business type.
- Document-heavy workflow.
- Reporting pain.
- Public contact path.
- Current operational context.

Do not infer sensitive personal details.

## Lead Fields

Capture:

- Name or company.
- URL/source.
- Contact path.
- Segment.
- Evidence.
- Pain signal.
- Fit score.
- Recommended angle.
- Outreach draft.
- Follow-up draft.
- Risk notes.
- Status.

## Status Values

Use these statuses:

- `new`
- `researched`
- `drafted`
- `needs_human_review`
- `approved`
- `sent_by_human`
- `replied_positive`
- `replied_negative`
- `skipped`

## Approval Packet Rules

Every approval packet must include:

- Why the lead was selected.
- What evidence supports the angle.
- The exact draft message.
- Any uncertainty.
- A recommendation: send, revise, or skip.
