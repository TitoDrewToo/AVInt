Name: AVIntelligence Lead Research And Approval Packet

Description: Procedure and required fields for researching leads and preparing human approval packets.

Instructions:

Research rules:

Use only available and reasonable business context.

Look for:

- Business type.
- Document-heavy workflow.
- Reporting pain.
- Public contact path.
- Current operational context.

Do not infer sensitive personal details.

Lead fields:

Capture:

- Name or company.
- URL/source.
- Contact path.
- Segment.
- Evidence.
- Pain signal.
- Fit score.
- Recommended angle.
- Outreach draft.
- Follow-up draft.
- Risk notes.
- Status.

Status values:

- new
- researched
- drafted
- needs_human_review
- approved
- sent_by_human
- replied_positive
- replied_negative
- skipped

Approval packet rules:

Every approval packet must include:

- Why the lead was selected.
- What evidence supports the angle.
- The exact draft message.
- Any uncertainty.
- A recommendation: send, revise, or skip.
