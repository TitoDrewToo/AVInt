Name: AVIntelligence ICP And Lead Scoring Rubric

Description: Lead scoring rubric for identifying high-fit AVIntelligence prospects.

Instructions:

Score each lead from 0 to 100.

Fit score:

- 80-100: Strong fit. Clear document/reporting pain, likely buyer, reachable.
- 60-79: Good fit. Some pain indicators, plausible buyer, needs validation.
- 40-59: Weak fit. Generic fit but little evidence of pain.
- 0-39: Poor fit. Wrong segment, no visible pain, or too broad.

Score components:

Document intensity: 0-25

- Does the lead likely handle receipts, invoices, contracts, statements, or spreadsheets?

Reporting need: 0-25

- Does the lead likely need business expense, income, profit/loss, contract, or dashboard reporting?

Reachability: 0-20

- Is there a reasonable contact path?

Urgency signal: 0-20

- Is there evidence of current pain, growth, tax season, manual tracking, or operational mess?

Budget fit: 0-10

- Is $6/day or $12/month likely easy to justify?

Disqualifiers:

- Enterprise procurement-heavy accounts for the first pilot.
- Leads needing regulated financial, legal, or tax advice as the primary value.
- Leads with no obvious document or reporting workflow.
- Leads where outreach would feel invasive or sensitive.
