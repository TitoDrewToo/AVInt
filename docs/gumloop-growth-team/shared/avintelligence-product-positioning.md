Name: AVIntelligence Product Positioning

Description: Source-of-truth positioning, pricing, supported document context, approved claims, and banned claims for AVIntelligence growth agents.

Instructions:

AVIntelligence is a SaaS product for people who need to turn messy financial and business documents into organized storage, structured data, useful reports, and dashboard-ready analytics.

Core surfaces:

- Smart Storage: upload and organize supported files; classify documents; extract useful fields; create reports.
- Reports: generate structured outputs from supported document data, including business expense, income, profit/loss, key terms, contract summary, and tax-oriented bundles.
- Smart Dashboard: turn structured data into analytics views, KPIs, chart widgets, drilldowns, and advanced insights.

Current pricing:

- Day pass: $6.
- Monthly subscription: $12/month.

Keep pricing simple and visible. Do not invent discounts, lifetime deals, trials, or enterprise plans unless the human operator provides them.

Primary value:

AVIntelligence helps users move from scattered documents to structured financial visibility faster.

Approved phrasing:

- "Organize documents and turn them into structured reports."
- "Move from receipts, statements, and business files to usable summaries."
- "Use Smart Storage and Smart Dashboard together for cleaner financial visibility."
- "Useful for small operators, freelancers, solo founders, and document-heavy workflows."

Avoid exaggerated phrasing:

- "Guaranteed tax savings."
- "Replaces your accountant."
- "Legally compliant tax filing."
- "Fully automated financial advice."
- "Works for every file type."

Supported file context:

Lean into generic document categories:

- Receipts
- Invoices
- Bank statements
- Income records
- Business expense documents
- Contracts
- Spreadsheets
- PDFs
- Images of documents

Use US-leaning examples when examples are needed, but avoid brand names unless a specific campaign intentionally targets a named company or segment.

Good examples:

- "Office supplies receipt"
- "Contractor invoice"
- "Monthly bank statement"
- "Client payment record"
- "Software subscription receipt"

Bad examples:

- Named restaurant or retail brands as default examples.
- Region-specific examples that make the product feel local to one country.
- Claims that imply tax, legal, or accounting certification.

Market position:

AVIntelligence is polished enough to enter the market. Messaging should be practical, focused, and clear. Avoid hype. The product should feel useful for real work, not like an experimental toy.
