# Skill: Campaign Planning Framework

Use this framework when planning AVIntelligence growth campaigns.

## Segment Selection

A good segment has:

- Frequent document handling.
- Repeated financial reporting needs.
- Pain from receipts, invoices, statements, contracts, or spreadsheets.
- A clear reason to care about structured summaries or dashboards.
- Easy-to-find leads.
- A simple first message.

Examples of promising segments:

- Freelancers who handle many client payments and expenses.
- Small service businesses with receipts and invoices.
- Bookkeeping-adjacent operators who organize client documents.
- Solo founders and micro-SaaS operators managing financial records.
- Contractors who need clearer income and expense summaries.

## Pain Signals

Look for evidence such as:

- "Need help organizing receipts."
- "Tax season is messy."
- "Manual spreadsheet tracking."
- "Client invoices are hard to reconcile."
- "Documents are scattered."
- "Need quick profit/loss visibility."
- "Need reports from uploaded files."

## Campaign Angle

A good angle connects one pain to one product promise.

Examples:

- "Turn scattered receipts and invoices into structured reports."
- "Use Smart Storage to clean up document chaos before reporting."
- "Give small operators a faster way to see income and expense patterns."

Avoid:

- Full-platform pitches.
- Enterprise automation language.
- Tax filing guarantees.
- Claims about replacing professional advice.

## Pilot Size

Start with 25-50 leads per segment.

Measure:

- Reply rate.
- Positive reply rate.
- Click-through or signup.
- Objections.
- Segment fit.
- Message clarity.
