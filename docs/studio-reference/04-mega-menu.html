Mega-menu — notch + morph

observed: gsap.com nav, 22 Aug 2026

The signature behaviour: open one trigger, then hover another and the panel
RESIZES and the notch TRAVELS. It does not close and reopen. Getting this
right depends on measuring the panel height at the TARGET width with the
transition suppressed — measuring mid-transition gives a wrapped, too-tall
content box and the panel opens with dead space at the bottom.
The notch is a sibling of the panel, not a child: the panel needs
overflow:hidden for the height tween, which would clip a child notch.
