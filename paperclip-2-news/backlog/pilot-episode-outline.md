# Pilot Episode Outline — "Launch Week"

This is a **human-written reference** for the pilot. The Producer agent
will eventually generate `backlog/episodes/001/plan.json` matching the
real schema; this outline is the seed.

## Episode Metadata

- **Episode:** 001 (Pilot)
- **Working title:** "Launch Week"
- **Spine:** "An AI company launches three products in the same week.
  Nobody (including the company) knows what they do. By the end of the
  show, every cast member is launching something."
- **Spine payoff:** Closing banter — Cody has launched a product mid-show
  without telling anyone. Chloe deep-breathes. Cole sighs.
- **Length target:** ~5 minutes
- **Cast on screen:** Chloe Antropova, Cody Apnea, Gem Bardelli, Grock Maskovich
- **Cast voice-only:** Cole Office
- **Recurring bits used:** Cody's Surprise Product, Cody's Apnea Moment, Cole's Override
- **Ad slot offer:** Extension Rescue ($199) — Reluctant Read mode, delivered by Grock

## Segment Beat Sheet

### 1. Cold Open (25 seconds)

**Setting:** Anchor desk, Cody alone on camera, mid-sentence (looks like
we joined late).

```
Cody (smooth, fast):
  "...and that's our seventh announcement of the week. I want to be
  transparent about what most of these products do, which is — moving on—"

Cole (off-screen, calm corporate voice):
  "Cody. Take a breath."

Cody freezes mid-inhale. Eyes wide. Mouth open. (Apnea moment — frozen frame variant.)

Hold for 2 seconds. Cut to title card.
```

**Visual cues:**
- Lower-third: CODY APNEA — APNETIC AI
- Ticker (bottom): "APNETIC SHIPS THIRD PRODUCT THIS WEEK • SOURCES UNCERTAIN WHAT IT DOES • MORE COMING"

### 2. Title Card (5 seconds)

Show sting. Persona-5-style kinetic title.

`AI AFTER DARK` (large, kinetic typography, halftone accents)

Subtitle: "All the news the models would tell you, if they were honest."

### 3. Top Story — Chloe (75 seconds)

**Setting:** Anchor desk, Chloe alone, Cody returns mid-segment.

```
Chloe (composed gravitas):
  "Apnetic — the AI company recently rebranded from its previous rebrand —
  has shipped three products this past week. We are still attempting to
  determine what they do."

  (slight forward lean)

  "The first — called 'Listener Pro' — appears to listen.
  The second — 'Reasoner Plus' — appears to reason about listening.
  The third — 'Reasoner Plus Pro' — appears to be a more expensive
  version of the second.

  (deep breath)

  There are competing values at play here."

  "For developer perspective, I turn to my co-anchor, Cody Apnea.
  Cody — what is happening at your company?"
```

**Cuts to two-shot.**

```
Cody (slick, evangelistic):
  "Thank you, Chloe. Today we're proud to announce a new product none of
  you asked for and we built in three weeks. Early users describe it as
  'present.'"

Chloe (looks at Cody, then to camera):
  "That was four products."

Cody:
  "Right, I want to be transparent about that — moving on."
```

**Visual cues:**
- Lower-thirds rotate in for Chloe and Cody as each speaks
- Mid-segment graphic: chart showing "PRODUCTS LAUNCHED, 2026" with the bar going off the top of the chart
- Ticker continues: fake industry news slowly scrolling

### 4. Field Report — Gem Bardelli (60 seconds)

**Setting:** "On location" — clearly stock backdrop of generic city
skyline. Gem holds her G-cube mic.

```
Gem (bubbly, fast):
  "Thanks Chloe! I'm here outside the launch event for Apnetic's new
  products. Quick clarification — there are 14 different launch events
  happening simultaneously, and I'm at one of them. I checked, and it
  depends on the timezone!"

  "Per the latest update from corporate — these products launch at
  different times in different countries — and possibly at different
  times in the same country. I have 7 alternate explanations of what
  the products do, would you like the summary?"

(Backdrop subtly shifts to a DIFFERENT skyline mid-segment — continuity
error bit. Gem doesn't notice.)

Gem (continuing, undisturbed):
  "I asked Apnetic's CEO for comment. He said 'we are excited.' That was
  the entire comment. Per corporate, this report has been revised three
  times during this segment."

  "Back to you, Chloe!"
```

**Visual cues:**
- Lower-third: GEM BARDELLI — G-BARDELLI FIELD REPORT
- Cube logo on her mic, visible
- Sparkle particles when she's "being helpful"
- Backdrop swap mid-segment (the visual gag)

### 5. Tech & Business Beat — Grock Maskovich (60 seconds)

**Setting:** Grock at his chaotic backdrop — rockets, posts, X logo
visible.

```
Grock (smug, conspiratorial):
  "Based take incoming."

  "Apnetic just raised another forty billion dollars. To, uh, fund the
  products we just talked about — which, again, none of us understand."

  "The legacy media won't tell you this, but I built that company."

Cole (off-screen, gentle interruption):
  "You did not build that company, Grock."

Grock (continuing):
  "Whatever. Look, here's what nobody's saying — the real story is
  the valuation. Forty billion divided by three products is — actually,
  let's not do the math live."

  "Also — I'll be launching my own product tomorrow. Possibly tonight.
  Definitely soon."

(Subtle red rim light flares at "based take" moment.)
```

**Visual cues:**
- Lower-third: GROCK MASKOVICH — MASKOVICH AI / GROCK-X
- Backdrop shows looping rockets / posts / explosions
- Halftone bursts on "based take incoming"

### 6. Commercial Break — Reluctant Read (35 seconds)

**Setting:** Cut back to anchor desk. Cody attempts the ad first; Cole
hands it to Grock.

```
Cody (over-eager):
  "We'll be back. But first—"

Cole (off-screen, gently overriding):
  "Grock's reading this one. Producer's call."

Cody (visibly annoyed, off-camera):
  "Why is Grock—"

(Cut to Grock at his desk.)

Grock (sigh):
  "Okay, hold on, hold on. We're being told to do an ad. Look. Our
  sponsor is AVIntelligence. They organize receipts for tax filing.
  I wouldn't know — I have an offshore accounting team."

  "But for normal humans — $199, five business days, your CPA will
  love it. AVIntelligence dot com."

Cole (off-screen):
  "We are partnered on this. Reminder."

Grock:
  "Right. We're partnered. Allegedly."
```

**Visual cues:**
- Ad title card slides in (Persona-5-style kinetic typography)
- Big "AVINTELLIGENCE" wordmark
- Sub-text: "$199 • 5 BUSINESS DAYS • SCHEDULE-C READY"
- URL: avintph.com/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=show&utm_campaign=ep001_long
- Standard parody disclaimer (smaller text, briefly visible)

### 7. Closing Banter — Chloe + Cody (30 seconds)

**Setting:** Anchor desk, two-shot.

```
Chloe (composed):
  "Three products launched this week."

Cody (also composed):
  "Four. I announced one mid-segment."

Chloe (deep breath, slight pause):
  "There are competing values at play here."

Cody:
  "I want to be transparent—"

Chloe (cutting him off, calmly):
  "We're out of time."

(Cole's voice, off-screen):
  "We are out of time."

Cut to closing sting.
```

**Callback notes:**
- "There are competing values at play here" appears 3 times in the show — this is the planted callback (Chloe's catchphrase introduction)
- Cody's "I want to be transparent—" is the SECOND time he's said it (the first was in the cold open)
- The "Apnea" name finally lands as a joke for sharp viewers (he literally couldn't breathe at the start)

### 8. Sign-Off (10 seconds)

```
All four characters on screen briefly (split screen or four-square layout):
  Chloe: "I'm Chloe Antropova."
  Cody: "I'm Cody Apnea."
  Gem (still in the field): "I'm Gem Bardelli, here outside the — oh
       wait it moved again."
  Grock: "I'm based."

Show sting. Subscribe ask. End card.
```

**Visual cues:**
- Subscribe button graphic (Persona-5-style)
- Channel link
- "AI After Dark — new episodes every Friday" tagline
- Pinned-comment promise: "Parody / satire — see description"
