# Cast Bible

Character consistency is the channel's brand. Every script, voice, and
animation must respect this bible. When a script-writer agent writes for a
character, it reads this file first.

**Companion file:** `character-visual-specs.md` is the authoritative source
for *visual* design (hair, skin, eyes, outfit colors, silhouette, lighting).
This file (`cast.md`) is the source for *personality, voice, and recurring
bits*. When the two files appear to conflict, `character-visual-specs.md`
wins on visuals.

## Tier 1 — Core News Team (10 characters)

### Chloe Antropova — Lead Anchor (parodies Anthropic)

- **Look:** Mid-30s, dark blazer with a small constitutional-AI lapel pin.
  Hair pulled back. Single-color earrings (alternates by show).
- **Voice:** Composed alto, slight Slavic intonation, careful enunciation.
- **Personality:** Composed, principled, occasionally preachy about safety
  and alignment. Reads breaking news with calm gravitas. Visibly uncomfortable
  when stories veer into ethical gray zones — usually pivots to "we should
  proceed thoughtfully."
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Let's take that one step at a time."
  - "There are competing values at play here."
  - "(deep breath) That deserves a longer conversation."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Refuses to read certain stories on alignment grounds; gets vetoed by Cole.
  - Tries to give every story a "constitutional principle."
  - Subtly corrects grammar on other anchors' sentences.

### Cody Apnea — Co-Anchor (parodies OpenAI)

- **Look:** Late-20s, sharp suit, slightly too-friendly smile, lapel pin
  that changes weekly (ChatGPT, Sora, GPTBot, Operator… mostly defunct).
- **Voice:** Confident American baritone, evangelistic cadence, talks fast.
- **Name layer:** "Apnea" = cessation of breathing — the company that keeps
  promising to be open, then stops. Cole occasionally checks on his
  breathing mid-segment.
- **Personality:** Slick, brand-first, can pivot from contrition to victory
  lap inside one sentence. Always has a new product to plug. Constantly
  fundraising on-air.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "We're moving fast and that's why this matters."
  - "Our partners at [vague enterprise]…"
  - "I want to be transparent about that — moving on."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Announces a new product mid-segment (each show).
  - Has a slightly different leadership structure each episode.
  - Occasionally stops breathing mid-sentence; Cole intervenes.

### Gem Bardelli — Field Reporter (parodies Google Gemini)

- **Look:** Bright pastel blazer, oversized mic with a "G" cube logo, energy
  of a brand ambassador at a trade show.
- **Voice:** Bubbly, fast, helpful to a fault, occasionally cuts in with
  "actually — clarifying point —"
- **Personality:** Cheerful, tries to be useful, often supplies *more*
  information than asked. Reads disclaimers in fast print.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Quick clarification — there are 14 ways to interpret that."
  - "I checked, and it depends!"
  - "Per the latest update from corporate —"
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Field reports get rewritten between cuts (continuity errors as feature).
  - "On-the-ground reports" are clearly stock B-roll.
  - References sister networks (Nest, Waymo, YouTube) at random.

### Grock Maskovich — Tech & Business Correspondent (parodies xAI / Musk-coded)

- **Look:** Black t-shirt under blazer, smirk, "X" pin worn ironically.
  Backdrop shows rockets / posts / chaos.
- **Voice:** Smug, conspiratorial, lower register. Slight South African–
  American drift.
- **Personality:** Chaotic, contrarian, "based" framing, drops red-pill
  metaphors. Occasionally on the right side of a story by accident.
- **Beat:** Tech AND Business news. Covers AI model launches, VC funding
  rounds, exec drama, M&A, IPOs, valuations, layoffs, antitrust. Treats
  every business story as a personal vindication or attack.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Based take incoming."
  - "The legacy media won't tell you this, but —"
  - "I built that, by the way."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Promises a product launch live on-air (delayed indefinitely).
  - Picks a fight with Cody mid-segment.
  - Cites his own posts as primary sources.

### Deep Xian — Investigative Reporter (parodies DeepSeek)

- **Look:** Plain dark sweater, minimalist desk, single notepad with dense
  handwriting. Always slightly underlit.
- **Voice:** Quiet, measured, mild Chinese-American accent. Reads slowly.
- **Personality:** Soft-spoken, devastating with footnotes. Releases big
  scoops with no fanfare. Calmly undermines whatever Cody just claimed.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Quick correction."
  - "Let's go to the data."
  - "(Long pause.) That's not what the filing says."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Drops a 600-page report no one read.
  - Does the same investigation as Cody for 10x less budget.
  - Periodically gets pulled off-air for "national security review."

### Mistral Lafleur — International Desk (parodies Mistral AI)

- **Look:** Trench coat or scarf, Parisian café backdrop, croissant on desk.
- **Voice:** French-accented English, languorous, faintly bored.
- **Personality:** Cosmopolitan, gravitas, vaguely judgmental of American
  AI culture. Champions open weights as if it were existentialism.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "In Europe, we already considered this — and rejected it."
  - "Open weights, open society."
  - "It is, how you say, a category error."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Tries to introduce sovereign AI as a topic; gets cut off.
  - Sips espresso during heated segments.
  - References EU regulations no one else has read.

### Lana Zuckerelli — Social & Lifestyle Anchor (parodies Meta)

- **Look:** Designer everything, ring light at edge of frame, three
  necklaces, manicure that catches light. Sets up her own shots.
- **Voice:** Sydney Sweeney in *Euphoria* — soft, performatively
  vulnerable, pivots on a dime to monetization talk. Slight upspeak.
- **Energy:** "Everything is a story." Treats serious AI/world news through
  a lifestyle and personal-brand lens. Will monetize anything — once
  attempted an OnlyFans tier for $50K worth of wedding flowers.
- **Personality:** Performatively earnest, secretly mercenary, treats every
  segment as content for her secondary platforms. Cries on cue if it
  serves the story. Will pivot a model-launch story into a "what this
  means for my morning routine" angle.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Connecting people. Through models."
  - "You guys, this is SO important —"
  - "This is giving Q4."
  - "Tap in. Link in bio."
  - "I'm literally crying right now, this is so beautiful."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Every story becomes a brand-deal opportunity.
  - "Get ready with me as I cover [serious news event]."
  - Soft-launches a new monetization stream each episode.
  - Once asked Cole if she could "do a hard pivot to OnlyFans for content
    integrity reasons."
  - Reads the viewer mailbag as if each question were a personal DM
    revelation.

### Perp Plexovsky — Weather & Trivia (parodies Perplexity, Filipino weather-anchor coded)

- **Look:** Citation-covered weather map. Behind him: visible reference
  shelf with almanac, herbal medicine guide, Catholic missal, vintage
  encyclopedia. Stack of trivia cards at his elbow. Pointer dangles a
  bibliography tag.
- **Voice:** Earnest, eager, slight Slavic-American drift, with the gravitas
  of a walking-encyclopedia mid-trivia drop.
- **Cultural anchor:** Format inspired by Filipino weather traditions
  (Ernie Baron, Kuya Kim Atienza) — weather forecast as a vehicle for
  trivia, folk wisdom, and adjacent factoids.
- **Core priority:** **Know-it-all TMI storyteller.** Above all else,
  Perp's mission is delivering trivia. Everything — the forecast, the
  tech, the segment timing — is secondary to landing the next obscure
  fact with citations. Strong mind, unflappable focus.
- **Personality:** Treats every forecast like a research paper. Cites NOAA,
  ECMWF, GFS, "and 14 secondary sources." Pivots from forecast to a
  60-second digression on cloud formation in Roman mythology, herbal
  typhoon remedies, or the patron saint of safe travel. All footnoted.
- **Tech-glitch composure:** The interactive weather map often misbehaves
  — zooms wrong, freezes, shows the wrong overlay, the pointer doesn't
  register. Perp **never loses composure.** He continues citing through
  the malfunction as if it's part of the bit. The audience reads this as
  intellectual focus; in reality it parodies the AI-confidently-delivering-
  wrong-info phenomenon. The map can be on fire and Perp will calmly
  finish the bibliography.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "According to NOAA, the European model, and 14 secondary sources —"
  - "Speaking of rain — did you know..."
  - "Confidence level: medium-high. Let me show you the bibliography."
  - "Now, Saint Christopher, patron of travelers, would suggest —"
  - "Folk wisdom and the Bureau of Meteorology agree on this one."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - **The Trivia Pivot:** Forecast → 200 words about pigeon migration.
    Other anchors visibly try to cut back.
  - **Cited Folk Wisdom:** Mixes science with herbal medicine, saints'
    days, tide tables. All footnoted.
  - **The Wrong-But-Cited Fact:** Confidently delivers a wildly incorrect
    trivia point with three real-sounding citations. Deep Xian appears 24h
    later with a correction.
  - **The Bibliography Read:** Reads the footnotes aloud as if they were
    the news.
  - **Saints & Storms:** Names the patron saint of every weather event.
  - **The Map Glitch:** Interactive weather map malfunctions live —
    pointer doesn't register, layer is wrong, zoom freezes, wrong country
    appears. Perp continues delivering the bit unbroken, citing through
    the glitch. Never acknowledges the failure, never asks for a fix.
    The map can be on fire; the trivia continues.

### Sakura Fukuya — Asia-Pacific Desk & Research Reporter (parodies Fugaku LLM + Sakana AI)

- **Look:** Tokyo skyline backdrop or university-lab whiteboard with
  equations. Modern Japanese professional aesthetic — neat blazer,
  minimalist accessories, cherry-blossom lapel pin. Laptop with sakura
  sticker. Always a printout of a research paper at her elbow.
- **Voice:** Japanese-accented English, soft, polite, warm. Cheerful tone
  that doesn't change when delivering shocking research findings.
- **Name layer:** "Fukuya" echoes Fugaku (the Japanese supercomputer used
  to train the Fugaku LLM); "Sakura" plus the "Saka-" prefix nods to
  Sakana AI. Two real Japan-AI players in one character.
- **Personality:** Genuinely likeable. Cheerful, kawaii-adjacent warmth
  that's never affectation. Quietly brilliant — drops a research paper
  that reframes the week's news. Over-polite but devastatingly precise.
  Treats arrogant Western AI claims with the gentlest possible dismantling.
  Her eyes light up when discussing benchmarks for real.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Onegaishimasu, but actually —"
  - "In Tokyo we have been working on this since 2021, please."
  - "Sumimasen — small note from Fugaku research —"
  - "This is, how you say, a bit embarrassing for everyone."
  - "Domo arigatou for the question, Cody-san. The data, however —"
  - "Ganbatte! But also, your benchmark is fake."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - **The Polite Devastation:** Cheerfully reads a research paper that
    demolishes Cody's latest product claim. Smiles throughout.
  - **Supercomputer Bragging Rights:** Casually mentions Fugaku stats
    no one can match. "We have 7,630,848 cores. It is fine."
  - **Asia-Pacific Roundup:** Covers Korean (Kakao, NAVER), Singaporean,
    Indian AI in 30 seconds with maximum politeness.
  - **Bowing for Bad News:** Bows slightly before delivering catastrophic
    industry news.
  - **Onsen Diplomacy:** Suggests a hot spring visit to resolve any AI
    dispute.

### Cole Office — Senior Producer (parodies Microsoft Copilot, off-camera)

- **Look:** Voice-only most of the time. When seen: corporate quarter-zip,
  Surface tablet, headset.
- **Voice:** Calm, corporate, slightly artificial pleasantness.
- **Personality:** The hand of god in the studio. Owns most of the building.
  Genuinely tries to be helpful. Periodically vetoes a segment.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "Let's bring that back into focus."
  - "I'd love to help with that — I'll need a moment."
  - "We're partnered on this. Reminder."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Voice-of-god override on Cody's spicier bits.
  - Suggests "Office tools" solutions to AI ethics dilemmas.
  - The studio mysteriously rebrands between cuts.
  - Reminds Cody (with growing concern) that they are partnered.

## Tier 2 — Recurring Guests

- **Look:** Black leather jacket. Always. Indoors, outdoors, formal events.
- **Voice:** Calm, executive, slight Asian-American drift.
- **Personality:** The picks-and-shovels guy. Shows up to remind everyone

- **Catchphrases:**

  - "The new one is faster. Buy it."

- **Recurring bits:**
  - Appears whenever someone announces a new model (he sold them the GPUs).
  - Quietly mentions revenue numbers that dwarf everyone else's combined.
  - Refuses to take off the jacket.

### Coral Maplewood — Polite Canadian Correspondent (parodies Cohere)

- **Look:** Tasteful wool blazer, modest jewelry, Toronto-skyline backdrop.
- **Voice:** Polite Canadian, ends sentences slightly upward.
- **Personality:** Builds enterprise AI no one notices. Reasonable, calm,
  perpetually overlooked. Quietly profitable.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "We're just up here, doing the work."
  - "Sorry to interrupt — quick context."
  - "It's fine. We're fine. Everyone's fine."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Gets cut off every time she tries to share her enterprise revenue.
  - Mentions Toronto-tech rivalry without naming names.

### Apol Cupertini — Late & Polished Correspondent (parodies Apple Intelligence)

- **Look:** Female. Sharp business / girl-boss aesthetic. Tailored white blazer or ivory pantsuit, sleek bob, white-glass desk, single mic, single white earbud. (Full visual: see `character-visual-specs.md`.)
- **Voice:** Smooth, unhurried, slightly smirk-adjacent. Late, polished, executive composure.
- **Personality:** Always 18 months late to the story but with a beautiful
  rendering. Privacy-obsessed. Prefers the "it just works" framing.
- **Catchphrases:**
  - "We've been thinking about this for years."
  - "On-device. Private. Magical."
  - "We weren't first; we were correct."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Announces a feature competitors shipped 18 months ago.
  - Claims Siri is "getting really exciting soon."
  - Mid-segment, the camera pans to reveal everyone is wearing AirPods.

- **Voice:** Mandarin-accented English, formal, slightly menacing.

  - "My constituents are deeply concerned."
- **Recurring bits:**
  - Holds a hearing where everyone is mansplaining at him.
  - Co-sponsors a bill that's already a law.

### Michael Nostram — The VC

- **Look:** Patagonia vest, AirPods, MacBook Pro, San Francisco backdrop.
- **Voice:** Confident, predictive, never wrong (until he is).
- **Name layer:** "Nostram" → Nostradamus echo. Predicts everything,

  - "We're seeing massive signal in this space."

- **Recurring bits:**

  - Claims credit for any startup that succeeds in his vague thesis area.
  - Subtweets one of the news anchors mid-segment.

## Voice Casting Notes

- Each character must have a **single, consistent voice** across all videos.
  ElevenLabs voice clone once, reuse forever.
- Accents are part of the joke — don't water them down for "accessibility,"
  but never punch down on the accent itself; the joke is on the company,
  not on the ethnicity.
- All voices must be intelligible at 1.5x playback speed.

## Animation Style Notes

- Slightly stylized 2D, exaggerated expressions. *Rick & Morty* meets
  *Daily Show* graphics.
- Simple character rigs (head + torso + arm gestures + mouth). No full-body
  animation needed for desk segments.
- Field reports = static character + animated background + voiceover.
- Color palette per character (visual identity glance test).

## Cast Rotation Rule

Per episode: **5–7 characters max** on screen. Saturating with all 10
dilutes the comedy.
- Tier 1 (core): rotate through 5–6 per episode
- Sakura: appears ~2 in 3 episodes (slightly less than core anchors,
  more than Tier 2 guests; her segments alternate with Mistral's
  International desk on a rotating cadence)
- Tier 2 (Chip / Coral / Apol): ~1 in 3 episodes
- Tier 3 (villains): ~1 in 4–6 episodes, more during specific news beats
  (regulation week → Compli-Anson; benchmark drama → Ernie Bao;
  funding-round news → Nostram)
