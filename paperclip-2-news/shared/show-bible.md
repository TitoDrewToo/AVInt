# Show Bible — Format, Segments, Recurring Bits

The show is fictional but follows real cable-news structure. Format is the
joke; the substance is the AI industry.

## Working Title

`AI After Dark` (proposed — easy to change)
Tagline: "All the news the models would tell you, if they were honest."

## Weekly Show Format (3–8 minutes)

| Segment | Length | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold Open | 15–30s | Whoever's news that week | Sets a joke before titles |
| Title Card | 5s | — | Channel branding sting |
| Top Story | 60–90s | Chloe (anchor desk) | Big AI story of the week |
| Field Report | 45–60s | Gem | On-the-ground (read: stock backdrop) |
| Tech & Business Beat | 45–60s | Grock | AI tech + funding rounds + exec drama / hot take |
| International Desk | 30–45s | Mistral *or* Sakura (alternates) | EU/sovereign-AI beat (Mistral) or Asia-Pacific roundup (Sakura) |
| Research Spotlight | 30–45s | Sakura (when paper warrants) | Drops a paper that reframes the week's news; runs ~1 in 2 episodes |
| Commercial Break | 30–45s | Ad Writer | In-show AVIntelligence ad |
| Investigative Spot | 45–60s | Deep | Quietly devastating |
| Weather & Trivia | 45–60s | Perp | Forecast → trivia digression (Baron/Atienza coded) |
| Lifestyle / Social | 45–60s | Lana | AI culture, monetization, "this is giving Q4" |
| Mailbag | 20–30s | Lana | Reads viewer questions as personal-DM revelations |
| Closing Banter | 20–30s | Chloe + Cody | One callback to earlier joke |
| Sign-off | 5–10s | All | Show sting + subscribe ask |

Total: ~5–7 minutes when fully populated. Trim segments for shorter weeks;
extend for big-news weeks. Pilot episode runs ~4–5 min; we earn the 8-min
slot over time.

## Daily Short Format (30–60 sec)

One-joke unit. Pick from:

1. **Hot Take Short:** A character reacts to an actual AI news headline.
2. **Recurring Bit:** A character does their signature shtick on a current event.
3. **Two-hander:** Two characters argue about the same story.
4. **Cold Open Tease:** Promo for the upcoming weekly show.
5. **Recap:** Best moment from the previous weekly show, recut.

Each short must:
- Land the joke in <5 seconds (algorithm hook)
- Have a clear "main character moment"
- End with a soft CTA (subscribe, watch the weekly, or AVInt mention)
- Be re-cuttable from existing weekly footage 60% of the time

## Recurring Bits (the brand)

These are the show's load-bearing comedy — same beat, different week. The
audience learns to expect them and laughs at the variation.

| Bit | Owner | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Cody's Surprise Product** | Cody | Mid-segment, announces a new product no one knew about |
| **Cody's Apnea Moment** | Cody | Stops breathing mid-sentence; Cole intervenes |
| **Chloe's Constitutional Concern** | Chloe | Refuses a story on alignment grounds, gets vetoed |
| **Deep's Footnote** | Deep | Quietly reads a contradictory citation |
| **Grock's Self-Cite** | Grock | Sources his own social posts |
| **Perp's Trivia Pivot** | Perp | Forecast → 200 words on pigeons |
| **Perp's Wrong-But-Cited Fact** | Perp | Defends a wrong factoid with three real citations |
| **Perp's Map Glitch** | Perp | Interactive map misbehaves; Perp continues citing unbroken |
| **Lana's Monetization Pitch** | Lana | Each story becomes a brand-deal opportunity |
| **Lana's "This Is Giving Q4"** | Lana | Reads serious news through a vibe filter |
| **Mistral's EU Regulation Drop** | Mistral | Mentions a real-feeling reg no one's heard of |
| **Sakura's Polite Devastation** | Sakura | Cheerfully demolishes Cody's product claim with a paper |
| **Sakura's Supercomputer Brag** | Sakura | Casually drops Fugaku core counts no one can match |
| **Gem's Continuity Error** | Gem | Same field report shifts between cuts |
| **Cole's Override** | Cole (VO) | Voice from above changes the segment |

Aim for **2–3 recurring bits per weekly show**, not all 12. Saturation kills
the joke; rotation keeps it alive.

## Tone Targets (the comedy DNA)

- **Anchorman:** Self-importance played straight, absurdity in earnest.
- **Daily Show with Jon Stewart:** News-format-as-vehicle, real industry
  beats hidden inside jokes, willingness to land a real point.
- **Family Guy cutaway energy:** Permission to break for one-line gags,
  visual non-sequiturs, callbacks. Sparingly.

What we do NOT want:
- ❌ Mean-spirited / cruel humor
- ❌ Punching down (at AI users, accents, individuals)
- ❌ Real-person impersonation that crosses into defamation
- ❌ Pure cynicism (the channel's voice is amused, not bitter)
- ❌ Inside-baseball humor only AI insiders get (50% should land for
  general audiences)

## Topic Domains (in priority order)

1. **AI industry news** — model launches, lawsuits, exec drama, benchmarks
2. **Real US news through an AI lens** — how would these characters cover
   politics, tech, business?
3. **Real world news through an AI lens** — geopolitics, especially where
   AI intersects (export controls, sovereignty, open-source)
4. **Philippine news (occasional)** — local angle when warranted; Perp's
   weather slot is a natural carrier for PH cultural references
5. **Internal AI culture** — benchmarks, evals, leaderboards, drama

Avoid:
- Specific personal scandals of named real executives (legal risk)
- Political endorsements (audience splits in half)
- Active legal cases where commentary could prejudice (be careful)

## Episode Arc Pattern (weekly)

A good weekly episode has a *spine* — one running thread that pays off in
the closing banter. Showrunner agent picks the spine each Monday.

Examples:
- Spine: "Everyone's launching a new model this week" → Cody announces 3,
  Grock promises one, Mistral judges them, Deep finds a benchmark each one
  failed.
- Spine: "Open vs closed weights debate flares" → Mistral & open-source
  defenders push, Chloe + Cody hedge, Grock flips on it twice.
- Spine: "AI hiring spree" → Cody steals Chloe's senior researcher mid-show,
  Grock offers everyone $300M, Cole reorgs the studio.
- Spine: "AI girlfriends are mainstream now" → Lana goes deep, Chloe is
  uncomfortable, Mistral judges American culture, Apol announces a polished
  late entry.

## House Style — Visual

- **Lower thirds:** Always show character name + parody-company (e.g.,
  "CHLOE ANTROPOVA — ANTHROPOMORPHIC NEWS")
- **Tickers:** Run real-feeling fake AI news at the bottom of every shot
- **Graphics:** Simple, slightly off (charts that don't quite add up, maps
  with one country missing)
- **Transitions:** Sharp cuts, no fancy wipes — Daily-Show energy
- **Sound stings:** Same 2-second sting between segments builds brand recall
- **Perp's Footnote Overlay:** When Perp speaks, footnote callouts appear
  at the edges of the frame, sometimes contradicting him

## House Style — Audio

- Music bed: low, jazz-news-coded
- Stings: programmatic via Remotion + free audio library
- Laugh track: NEVER. The jokes have to earn it.

## Continuity Rules

- **Characters never break the fourth wall** unless it's a deliberate bit
- **Characters reference past episodes** (callbacks build viewership)
- **The fictional studio universe is internally consistent** — same set,
  same desk, same backdrop unless we cut to "field report"

## Cast Introduction Strategy

Roll the team out gradually. Each new episode adds one new face. Audience
gets time to learn each character without overwhelm.

**Episode 1 (Pilot):**
- **Chloe Antropova** — anchor desk
- **Cody Apnea** — co-anchor
- **Gem Bardelli** — field reporter
- **Grock Maskovich** — Tech & Business beat
- **Cole Office** — voice-only producer override

**Episode 2:** + **Sakura Fukuya** (Asia-Pacific desk debut — "the new
correspondent" cold-open beat)

**Episode 3:** + **Mistral Lafleur** (International desk — French
correspondent break-in via satellite)

**Episode 4:** + **Deep Xian** (Investigative — quietly walks on, drops
a footnote, walks off)

**Episode 5:** + **Lana Zuckerelli** (Lifestyle — debut as "our new
social desk" — Cody is visibly threatened)

**Episode 6:** + **Perp Plexovsky** (Weather & Trivia — debuts with a
broken interactive map, calmly cites through it)

By episode 6: full Tier 1 cast on the show. Tier 2 guests (Chip, Coral,
Apol) and villains (Ernie, Compli-Anson, Nostram) join organically when
news warrants.

## Pilot Episode Structure (Episode 1)

The first episode should:

1. Establish the news team — only 4 main characters + voice-of-Cole
2. Introduce them via cold open + first 2 segments
3. Land 1 strong recurring bit (Cody's Surprise Product is the natural pick)
4. Run the AVInt ad (defines the show's commerce model from day 1)
5. End with a callback to the cold open

Tight cast = tight pilot. Future characters appear per the introduction
schedule above.
