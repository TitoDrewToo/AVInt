Pill button — inset fill

observed: gsap.com “Get GSAP”, 22 Aug 2026

The fill expands INSIDE the stroke, leaving a 2px gap, so the stroke never
moves and the outline reads as a fixed frame. The primary variant is FILLED
at rest — an accent-coloured outline on a dark ground is low-contrast and
reads as a warning. Hover inverts the fill, keeping the same mechanic.
