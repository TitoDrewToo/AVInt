Split-text line reveal

observed: gsap.com “Why GSAP®”, 22 Aug 2026

Masked per LINE, not per word — the difference is the whole effect. Lines
can only be found after layout, so: wrap each word, read offsetTop, group
words that share one, then rewrap as masked lines. Rebuild on resize.
Must wait for document.fonts.ready or the line breaks are measured against
the fallback face and regroup wrongly once the webfont lands.

The trap: overflow:hidden on the line box shears the descenders off every
g, y, p and j. Pad the mask down and pull it back up by the same amount.
